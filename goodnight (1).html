🌙   A Goodnight Wish   🌙
Dearest Angel,

As the night wraps itself around the world, know that someone out here is thinking of you — hoping your pillow is soft, your mind is quiet, and your heart is at peace.

You deserve a night full of the most beautiful dreams. Let go of everything heavy. Let the stars keep watch over you while you rest.

You are so cherished. Never forget that.

Goodnight Angel 🥰
